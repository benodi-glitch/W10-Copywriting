Copywriting Lab: From Picture to Persuasive Message

Choose one business or product. Define your audience, select a structure, write each part of the copy, and check whether your claims are credible.

1

Choose one business or product

Select ONE picture. Work independently and complete all the steps below.

Evidence rule: These are fictional practice concepts. The picture and product name do not prove any specific performance, price, award, review, or guarantee. You may propose a benefit, but do not present unverified details as facts. If evidence is missing, state what evidence the business would need to collect.
2

Set the strategy

Decide who the copy is for and what you want the reader to do.

Choose one copy structure

Choose the structure that best fits your audience and message. It is a guide, not a guarantee of success.

3

Build the parts of your copy

Write each element. Keep the audience and chosen structure in mind.

4

Check proof and credibility

Make the ad believable without inventing information.

5

Close the copy and assemble the final version

Finish with a clear action and a memorable line, then combine the parts into one polished ad.

6

Self-review and sentence check

Read the entire advertisement once before you finish.

S = subject • V = verb • O = object • A = adverbial (when, where, how, or why). An element may be absent.
